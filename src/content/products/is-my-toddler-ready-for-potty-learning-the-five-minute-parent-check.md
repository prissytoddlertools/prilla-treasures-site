Potty learning can feel confusing when parents are unsure whether to begin, wait or change their approach.

The Five Minute Parent Check was created to replace pressure with observation. It helps parents notice repeated readiness patterns, prepare a clearer routine and choose one manageable next step.

The goal is not to rush the child. The goal is to help the parent begin with greater clarity and confidence.
