Bedtime often becomes difficult when everyone is already tired. Repeated requests, changing rules and long explanations can turn a familiar routine into a nightly negotiation.

Bedtime Without the Battle was created around a more useful question: how can the evening become clearer and more predictable before resistance begins?

The RESTED approach combines a realistic rhythm, a calmer environment, the same short sequence, a small moment of connection, one consistent parent-presence plan and a simple way to review what happened. The goal is not to promise perfect sleep or a tear-free night. It is to give parents a practical plan they can repeat, observe and adjust one step at a time.
