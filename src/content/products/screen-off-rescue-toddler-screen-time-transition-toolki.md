Screen time often begins because a parent needs a few minutes and ends when everyone is already stretched. Repeated warnings, bargaining and long explanations can make the transition even harder.

Screen-Off Rescue was created around a different question: how can we make the entire transition clearer before the screen even begins?

The PAUSE approach combines a visible stopping point, consistent language, helpful device settings, prepared next-step choices and a simple response ladder for big feelings. It is practical, respectful and free from blame. The goal is not a perfectly happy ending every time. The goal is a more predictable routine and a steadier response for the adult to follow.
