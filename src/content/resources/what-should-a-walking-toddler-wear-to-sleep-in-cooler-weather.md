**What Is a Walker Sleep Sack?**

A walker sleep sack is wearable sleepwear designed for children who are becoming more mobile.

Instead of having both legs enclosed together, the child has separate leg sections.

That can make bedtime more practical for toddlers who want to walk around before bed, climb into a parent’s lap, choose a bedtime book, move between rooms, or simply refuse to stay still.

For an active toddler, that extra freedom can make the difference between sleepwear that feels restrictive and sleepwear that fits their stage of development. The idea is simple: Warmth while still allowing movement.

Before Buying: Do Not Choose TOG Based Only on the Word “Winter”

You will often see sleep sacks labeled with numbers such as 0.5 TOG, 1.0 TOG, 2.5 TOG, and 3.5 TOG.

TOG is a measure of thermal insulation.

Generally: The higher the TOG number, the warmer the sleepwear.

Kaiya Baby currently gives these general room-temperature ranges:

- 0.5 TOG: approximately 24–27°C / 75–80°F
- 1.0 TOG: approximately 20–24°C / 68–75°F
- 2.5 TOG: approximately 16–20°C / 61–68°F
- 3.5 TOG: approximately 14–16°C / 57–61°F

This is why you should not automatically think: “Winter is coming, so I need the warmest sleep sack.”

If your child’s room stays warm because of heating, a lighter TOG may be more appropriate.

The best place to start is the temperature of the room where your child actually sleeps.

A bedroom at 21°C during winter does not automatically need the same sleepwear as a bedroom that drops to 16°C.
