Start with a calmer first step

Use this short readiness check before you begin potty learning. It helps you notice your toddler’s current signals, prepare the environment and choose a gentle next step without pressure.

How to use it

Read through the check, note what you are already seeing and choose one small preparation step. Readiness can develop over time. You do not need to rush.

Important note

This resource is for general education and routine support. It does not diagnose or treat a medical or developmental condition. Speak with a qualified professional if you have concerns about your child’s health, development or safety.
